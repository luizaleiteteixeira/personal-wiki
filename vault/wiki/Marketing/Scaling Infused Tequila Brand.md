# Scaling Infused Tequila Brand

Diageo acquired a successful infused tequila brand and must now decide on growth strategies to balance the founder's vision with the corporation's mandate for scale. Potential paths include product innovation, market expansion, and brand positioning shifts.

## Key points

- Growth options include product innovation, new sizes, Ready-to-Drink (RTD) ventures, and on-premise expansion.
- Brand positioning faces tension between maintaining the original female focus and appealing to a broader market, including men.
- International expansion is possible, though the majority of tequila consumption remains in the U.S. and Mexico.
- Media buying choices involve high-CPM targeted campaigns versus scalable programmatic buying.
- The founder's vision prioritizes brand story and engagement, while Diageo drives the need for scale and portfolio leverage.
- Competitive pressure exists from established brands like Cuervo 1800 and private labels.
