# Sources of Market Differentiation

Differentiation is a strategy that can be applied through any of the marketing mix elements, not solely the product itself. Businesses can achieve differentiation by focusing on various aspects of their offering or execution.

## Key points

- Differentiation can stem from any of the 4Ps.
- Ways to achieve differentiation include product features, imagery, packaging, and user experience (UX).
- Distribution channels can also be a source of differentiation, as seen with In-N-Out and Zara.
- Companies can also differentiate by removing features, a concept referred to as "IKEA subtraction."
