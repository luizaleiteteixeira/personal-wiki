# Flag Manufacturer Growth Strategy

AFS, a premium flag manufacturer, seeks ways to expand beyond its main business by offering related products and services. Successful growth involves deepening the emotional connection and identity expression rather than simply adding unrelated items.

## Key points

- Growth options include new materials, accessories, premium finishes, or adjacent patriotic products.
- Augmentation, such as premium stitching or heritage tags, helps create value perception in symbolic categories.
- Extensions must align with the core emotional job-to-be-done, which is often patriotism or identity.
- Cannibalization is not an issue if the total portfolio value increases.
- Growth is achieved by offering more ways for customers to express their identity.
