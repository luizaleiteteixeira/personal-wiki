# Vans Sustainability Strategy

Vans is evaluating whether to deepen its sustainability efforts to resonate with Gen Z consumers. The challenge lies in aligning sustainability with the brand's existing identity rooted in rebellion and action sports.

## Key points

- Cause initiatives are only effective when they reinforce the brand's existing meaning.
- While sustainability can attract customers, it also risks diluting the brand's core identity.
- Consumer claims regarding valuing sustainability do not always match actual purchasing behavior.
- Sustainability efforts should function as an augmentation layer rather than replacing the brand's core identity.
