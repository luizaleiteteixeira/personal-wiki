# Distribution Channel Structures

Distribution channels use intermediaries to connect producers with buyers, adding value through functions like access and logistics. The structure can range from conventional independent partners to integrated Vertical Marketing Systems.

## Key points

- Intermediaries add value through Access, Storage, Logistics, and Aggregation.
- Conventional channels feature independent partners, whereas VMS involves alignment through ownership or contract.
- Disintermediation (DTC) or reintermediation (platforms) offers greater process control if intermediaries add value.
- Omnichannel requires segmenting consumers based on their shopping attitudes rather than demographics.
- Channel conflict can be horizontal (same level) or vertical (within the channel).
- Push strategy involves intermediaries actively pushing the product, while Pull relies on consumer demand for the brand.
