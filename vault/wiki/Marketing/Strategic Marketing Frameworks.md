# Strategic Marketing Frameworks

This section outlines various frameworks useful for strategic planning, competitive analysis, and product management. Each framework provides a specific output depending on whether the focus is on market opportunity, product development, or competitive positioning.

## Key points

- The Marketing Process (5Cs) is used for strategic planning to identify opportunities and positioning.
- The Positioning Map aids in competitive analysis by providing a visual comparison of brands.
- Product strategy can be guided by the Product Levels model, which prioritizes features from core to potential.
- The Product Life Cycle is employed for portfolio management to determine stage-specific strategies.
- Porter's Advantage helps in competitive strategy by forcing a choice between cost leadership and differentiation.
- The Messaging Hierarchy structures brand communication from differentiators down to the core value and slogan.
- The BCG Matrix is used for portfolio evaluation to guide growth and share strategy.
- Distribution design can be addressed using Channel Alternatives, comparing VMS, conventional, and DTC models.
