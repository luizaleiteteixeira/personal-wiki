# Sources of Competitive Advantage

Competitive advantage in marketing can stem from several sources, including product differentiation, brand strength, and operational efficiencies. Marketing activities play a crucial role in driving and sustaining this advantage.

## Key points

- Sources of advantage include differentiation, brand equity, network effects, distribution power, cost structure, and speed to innovate.
- Marketing contributes to competitive advantage through category creation, mental availability, emotional attachment, and switching costs.
