# Market Segmentation Strategy

Segmentation is the process of dividing a total market into smaller, meaningful, and actionable groups. This process helps identify and analyze potential markets before selecting a specific target.

## Key points

- The process involves dividing the universe into actionable, meaningful groups.
- Best practice suggests starting with occasions and benefits sought before refining with demographics, psychographics, behaviors, loyalty, and attitudes.
- Effective segmentation must be homogeneous within segments, heterogeneous between segments, substantial, and reachable.
- Examples include Levi's segments or Starbucks segmenting by beverage ritual.
