# Levels of Product Offering

This model describes the different layers of value a product provides to the customer, starting from the fundamental need it fulfills up to extra services. Each level builds upon the previous one, adding features or services to enhance the offering.

## Key points

- Core level represents the basic need the product satisfies.
- Expected level refers to the physical product itself.
- Augmented level includes added services like warranties or loyalty programs.
- The Potential level represents the next evolution of the product.
- Augmentation can involve adding features, multiplying the product portfolio, or removing elements.
