# Marketing Foundational Concepts

Marketing is the process of creating, communicating, and delivering value to a specific target audience. It is fundamentally customer-centric, differing from a product-first approach. A brand, conversely, is the combination of a promise, expectation, and experience held in the consumer's mind.

## Key points

- Marketing focuses on the customer's needs first, rather than starting with the product.
- Brand equals promise plus expectation plus experience.
- Brand Equity involves mental associations, perceived value, and loyal users.
- The 5C's framework includes Company, Customer, Competitor, Collaborators, and Context.
- The sales journey can be mapped through a Funnel: Awareness to Purchase.
- Brand architecture types include House of Brands, Branded House, and Hybrid models.
- Marketing involves creating and delivering value to satisfy customer needs.
- Value Proposition is the promised set of benefits, both physical and emotional, that meet customer needs.
- Strategy defines the vision for creating customer value, while tactics are the specific activities in the marketing plan.
- The Marketing Process uses five Cs: Customer, Company, Competition, Collaborators, and Context.
