# Product Differentiation And Positioning

Positioning refers to the specific mental space a product holds in consumers' minds compared to rivals. Successful differentiation requires that any differences offered are valuable to the consumer, whether through problem-solving or providing delight.

## Key points

- Positioning is the product's place in consumers' minds relative to competitors.
- Differentiation can stem from cost, physical product attributes, or emotional imagery.
- Differences must be meaningful to consumers to provide competitive advantage.
- Product levels range from Core functionality to Potential future possibilities.
- Augmentation strategies include adding features, expanding the portfolio, or taking elements away.
