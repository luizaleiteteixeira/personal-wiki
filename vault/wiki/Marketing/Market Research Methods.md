# Market Research Methods

Market research bridges the gap between a business decision and the information required to support it. It involves various types and methods depending on the research objective.

## Key points

- The research problem defines what information is needed to address the business decision.
- Research types include Exploratory (understanding a situation), Evaluative (go/no-go decisions), and Tracking (monitoring trends).
- Quantitative methods use large samples like surveys, while Qualitative methods use in-depth approaches like interviews.
- Primary research is collected by the researcher, whereas secondary research already exists.
- Buyer behavior involves System 1 (fast, intuitive) and System 2 (slow, deliberate) thinking.
- Motivation can be intrinsic (enjoyment) or extrinsic (rewards).
- A decision problem is the business decision that needs to be made.
- The research problem defines the information needed to help make that decision.
- Research can be Exploratory ("What's happening?"), Evaluative ("Go/no-go decision"), or Tracking (monitoring trends).
- Behaviors can be Actual (purchases), Observed (video), or Reported (surveys).
