# Brand Positioning Strategy

Positioning refers to the specific place a brand holds within the consumer's mind. Effective positioning must be simple, relevant, and centered around value to resonate with the target audience.

## Key points

- Great positioning requires a clear benefit, target, frame of reference, differentiation, and reason to believe.
- A positioning statement follows the format: For [target], [brand] is the [frame of reference] that [primary benefit], because [reason to believe].
- Apple positions itself around creativity, simplicity, and a premium experience.
- IKEA positions itself by offering functional design at accessible prices.
- Nike positions itself as a performance brand helping athletes push their limits through innovation and data.
