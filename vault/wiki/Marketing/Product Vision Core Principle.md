# Product Vision Core Principle

Product vision requires an obsessive focus on both the product design and the overall user experience. Technology itself should be viewed as a tool to achieve a greater goal, rather than being the end product.

## Key points

- Technology serves as a means to an end.
- Success lies at the intersection of liberal arts and technology.
- Brand and product excellence stems from uncompromising attention to detail.
- Simplicity and elegance are crucial elements of successful products.
