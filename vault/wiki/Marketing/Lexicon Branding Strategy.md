# Lexicon Branding Strategy

Naming strategy involves creating a brand name that is memorable and legally protectable. A good name must be distinctive, easy to pronounce, and capable of evoking positive associations for the brand.

## Key points

- A good brand name must be distinctive, easy to say, and scalable.
- It should evoke desired associations and be legally and linguistically ownable.
- Types of names include descriptive, suggestive, empty vessel, and experiential.
- Examples of types include Whole Foods (Descriptive), Airbnb (Suggestive), and Kodak (Empty vessel).
