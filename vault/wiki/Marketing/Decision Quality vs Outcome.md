# Decision Quality vs Outcome

The quality of a decision should be evaluated based on the reasoning and information used at the time, rather than solely on the final outcome. People often confuse the two, leading to biases like hindsight bias.

## Key points

- Quality of a decision is not the quality of its outcome.
- Resulting occurs when decisions are evaluated only on their outcomes.
- Hindsight bias involves feeling like one "should have known" after a negative outcome.
- Luck and skill both influence outcomes, even in high-stakes scenarios.
- Fast, automatic thinking (System 1) and slow, deliberative thinking (System 2) are both involved in decisions.
- Decisions should be reviewed based on the reasoning and information available, not just success.
