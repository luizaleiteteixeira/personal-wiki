# Pulse Fitness Segment Evaluation

Pulse Fitness analyzed three customer segments to determine which offered the best long-term value. The strongest segment was identified not by size or intuition, but by the highest Customer Lifetime Value (CLV).

## Key points

- The goal is to find the segment with the highest CLV.
- Larger segments do not automatically equate to better targets.
- Segmentation must be linked to economic outcomes like retention and visit frequency.
- The most strategic segment is the one that can be served profitably.
- Customers buy solutions, not just memberships.
