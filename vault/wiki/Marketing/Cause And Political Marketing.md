# Cause And Political Marketing

This section outlines considerations for cause marketing, political marketing, and managing boycotts. It emphasizes tailoring messages to the specific target audience and understanding the difference between stated attitudes and actual behavior.

## Key points

- Political marketing suggests targeting segments with fewer than 10% potential switchers.
- Messages should be kept simple and focused on addressing problems.
- Negative advertisements can be effective due to lack of standards and rapid dissemination.
- For boycotts, a three-part strategy is recommended: admit the problem, provide clear corrective steps, and reinforce positive brand imagery.
- In sustainability marketing, the target audience is crucial, and brand positioning versus augmentation must be considered.
