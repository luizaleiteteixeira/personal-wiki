# Nike Consumer Direct Offense

Nike is transitioning toward a Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) model, which allows it to control data, margins, and brand experience by decreasing reliance on wholesale partners. This shift, however, introduces execution challenges related to stock availability, partner pushback, and physical presence.

## Key points

- DTC increases margins but also increases operational complexity.
- Wholesale partners offer reach, discovery, and convenience to the brand.
- A change in channel strategy must align with the company's operational readiness.
- Channel strategy is a value chain decision, not solely a marketing function.
- Removing intermediaries can boost efficiency but may destabilize the entire ecosystem.
- Continuing DTC offers full control over brand image, pricing, and consumer experience, alongside direct access to consumer data.
- Traditional wholesale distribution provides higher customer reach, faster inventory turnover, and shared logistics costs.
- The recommended strategy is to remain DTC-focused while strengthening selected wholesale partnerships to achieve balance.
- DTC targets digitally engaged, lifestyle-oriented consumers with curated, high-innovation products.
