# Pricing Strategies And Models

Pricing decisions involve balancing cost structures with consumer perception of value. Successful pricing requires selecting a strategy and model that aligns with market dynamics and desired business outcomes.

## Key points

- Value-based pricing should be the starting point, followed by checking if costs allow for a margin.
- Elasticity of demand is influenced by category, competitors, customer segments, and substitutes.
- Penetration pricing uses a low initial price to achieve rapid market entry, though sustaining it can be difficult.
- Skimming involves setting a high initial price to capture revenue, requiring careful timing relative to competitors.
- Pricing models include Subscription, Freemium, Bundling, Captive products, and Surge pricing.
- Pricing can achieve positioning, differentiation, barriers to entry, and accelerating purchase cycles.
