# Marketing Imagination Concepts

Successful marketing involves redefining the problem being addressed rather than simply selling a product. True market differentiation arises from imagination and the ability to intentionally diverge from market norms.

## Key points

- Marketing is about redefining the problem, not selling products.
- Categories can become limiting when defined too narrowly.
- True differentiation stems from imagination, not just product features.
- Markets naturally trend toward sameness unless a brand actively diverges.
- Consumers must grasp your frame of reference before understanding your differentiation.
- Decisions in business are probabilistic, and outcomes do not validate the decision quality.
