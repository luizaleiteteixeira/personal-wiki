# Promotion and Communications Strategy

Promotion objectives must be measurable and focused on driving specific behavioral changes. Effective communication requires aligning messaging with the consumer's perspective and utilizing a strong strategic foundation.

## Key points

- Promotion objectives must be measurable and tied to specific behavior change.
- The communication process follows stages like Awareness, Interest, Desire, and Action.
- A single-minded message should be simple, straightforward, and easy to remember.
- Emotional messaging often drives consumers more effectively than purely rational messaging.
- A creative brief translates marketing strategy into a clear communication direction, defining elements like the Target and SMIP.
- Brand building is a long-term activity focused on loyalty, whereas promotion drives short-term action.
- Drivers for rebranding include an evolving category, a need to simplify the brand story, or outdated imagery.
- Good messaging must be rooted in deep customer insight.
- A single narrative should be maintained across all customer touchpoints.
- Emotional resonance combined with clarity is crucial for successful messaging.
