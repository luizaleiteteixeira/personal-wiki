# Tepper School Rebranding Case

The Tepper School underwent a complete branding refresh to clearly define its identity at the intersection of analytics and leadership. The process required aligning messages across all stakeholders and distilling the core identity into a concise slogan.

## Key points

- Rebranding success hinges on achieving clarity in the message, rather than superficial elements like logos or colors.
- A slogan is only effective if it captures the strategic essence of the organization.
- A strong brand identity requires the ability to unite diverse stakeholders under one narrative.
- Organizations must simplify complex identities into a single, coherent narrative.
- The initial perception of Tepper was "future-oriented" and "innovation," but the messaging lacked clarity.
- Research showed Tepper was viewed as small, collaborative, and rigorous in analytics.
- The untapped positioning identified was around becoming "data-driven leaders."
- The messaging hierarchy moved from differentiators to customer benefits, culminating in a core value.
