# Category Management Strategies

Category management involves strategic decisions regarding a product portfolio, including choices about which products to maintain and how to grow the offering. It requires evaluating various extension strategies and the potential impact of product lines.

## Key points

- Decisions can involve disinvesting in some products while focusing on higher revenue generators.
- Strategies must balance between breadth and depth of the product offering.
- Decisions include whether to pursue line extensions or brand extensions.
- Cannibalization must be evaluated when making portfolio choices.
- Brand architecture plays a role in these management decisions.
