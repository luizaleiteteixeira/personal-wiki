# Go-To-Market Strategy

Go-to-Market (GTM) strategy defines how a product is introduced, distributed, communicated, and scaled to its target audience. It involves several core pillars necessary for a successful market entry.

## Key points

- GTM encompasses introduction, distribution, communication, and scaling of a product.
- Core pillars include target clarity, positioning plus messaging, and channel strategy.
- A launch plan, whether phased or mass, is a critical component.
- Feedback loops are necessary for ongoing market presence.
- Examples include launching via niche influencer communities or starting in cafés before mass retail.
