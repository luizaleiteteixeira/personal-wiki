# Teams Versus Zoom Comparison

The comparison between Microsoft Teams and Zoom illustrates how platform dynamics can outweigh individual product advantages. Early success can be driven by simplicity, but long-term success often depends on ecosystem integration and bundling.

## Key points

- Zoom gains early success through simplicity and frictionless onboarding.
- Teams achieves long-term success through bundling, integration, and ecosystem strength.
- The case demonstrates that platform logic often dominates product-level advantages.
- Ease of use drives early adoption, akin to System 1 processing.
- Bundling, such as being "free with Office," aids in forced trial and retention.
- Ecosystem strength proves more valuable than a standalone product.
- Zoom initially gained default status as the video conferencing tool during COVID.
- Microsoft Teams competes by leveraging bundling with Office 365 and enterprise integration.
- Strategic considerations include Zoom's first-mover advantage against Microsoft's bundling power.
- The competition pits Zoom's ease of use against Teams' deep integration capabilities.
