# Porters Competitive Advantage Framework

This framework assesses a company's ability to achieve a competitive advantage by looking at the attractiveness of the market and the company's relative position within it. Success depends on effectively managing both the market structure and the company's chosen path to differentiation.

## Key points

- Strategy involves two components: structure attractiveness and relative position.
- Structure attractiveness evaluates whether the category or market itself is appealing.
- Relative position determines if the company can compete successfully in the market.
- Differentiation can be achieved through cost leadership or product differentiation.
- Small and large companies must tailor their strategies to leverage their core competencies and resources.
