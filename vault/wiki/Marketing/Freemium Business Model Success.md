# Freemium Business Model Success

The freemium model offers basic features for free while gating advanced functionalities behind a paid subscription. Success hinges on balancing the free offering to drive traffic while clearly communicating the value proposition of the premium tier.

## Key points

- The free tier acts as an acquisition engine, aiming for a moderate conversion rate.
- The ideal conversion rate ranges between 2% and 5% when scaling successfully.
- The offering must be clearly understood by customers to drive conversions.
- Early adopters are typically less price-sensitive compared to later users.
- Free users can contribute significantly by becoming evangelists through referrals.
- Ongoing innovation in the premium features is necessary to sustain later conversions.
