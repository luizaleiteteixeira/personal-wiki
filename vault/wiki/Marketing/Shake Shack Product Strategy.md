# Shake Shack Product Strategy

Shake Shack faces a strategic decision regarding its core offering amidst shifting consumer preferences toward alternatives to beef. The challenge is balancing brand authenticity with the need to innovate and appeal to broader market trends.

## Key points

- Innovation should extend the brand, not contradict it.
- Even declining categories can remain central if the brand identity is strong.
- Strategic growth involves adjacencies like chicken, veggie, or limited editions.
- Customers are drawn to the burger ritual, not solely the food itself.
- The brand was initially built on an "enlightened" approach using hormone-free and antibiotic-free beef.
- The company needed to address market trends concerning health and environmental impact.
- Key strategic questions included how to grow while preserving the core burger positioning.
- Decisions had to be made regarding product innovation (e.g., chicken, plant-based options) versus maintaining focus.
