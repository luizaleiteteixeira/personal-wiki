# Selecting Target Markets

Targeting involves deciding which specific group of customers to pursue. This decision is made to make marketing efforts more efficient and effective by tailoring messages.

## Key points

- Targeting variables include demographics, psychographics, behavioral, geography, and broader buyer behavior.
- The Brand Development Index (BDI) and Category Development Index (CDI) help assess market maturity.
- High BDI and low CDI suggest a maintenance or defensive strategy is needed.
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) is the true measure of a customer's value.
- CLV is calculated as (Margin x Expected Lifetime) - Customer Acquisition Cost.
- Influencers, decision makers, and purchasers can be different, especially in B2B settings.
