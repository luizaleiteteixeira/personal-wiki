# Cause Marketing Strategy

Brands must integrate sustainability and social values into their market presence to remain relevant. A successful strategy involves a deliberate, multi-step approach to address issues while maintaining a positive brand image.

## Key points

- Brands need to address sustainability and social values to succeed in the current market.
- The three-part strategy includes admitting a problem, outlining clear steps, and reinforcing positive imagery.
- Brand vulnerability arises when the company's positioning does not match its actual actions.
- Consumer behavior is increasingly influenced by alignment with brand values.
- Examples of brand risk include Bud Light, Planet Fitness, McDonald's, and Starbucks.
