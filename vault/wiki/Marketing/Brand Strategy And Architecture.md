# Brand Strategy And Architecture

A brand is a collection of symbols and experiences that exists separately from the product itself. Brand equity represents the valuable assets a brand possesses, with loyal customers being the most crucial asset for sustained business success.

## Key points

- A brand is defined by symbols, images, logo, name, and in-store experience.
- Brand equity includes assets like awareness, quality perceptions, and loyal customers.
- Brand architecture types include House of Brands, Endorsed Brands, Sub-brands, and Branded House.
- Line extensions involve new products within the same category, while brand extensions venture into new categories.
- Category management illustrates the core, expected, augmented, and potential offerings against competition.
- Building a unique brand identity comes from small, consistent choices.
- These choices accumulate to form the overall brand position.
- Focus on achieving a distinctive market presence.
