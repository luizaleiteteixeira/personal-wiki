# A Whole New Mind

In an abundance economy, services that are highly experiential and emotionally driven command greater value. While analytical skills are still needed, they alone are not enough to succeed.

## Key points

- High-touch, design-centric, emotionally intelligent services command premium value in an abundance economy.
- Left-brain analytical skills are necessary but insufficient for modern success.
- Differentiation in marketing now stems from design, brand story, and emotional resonance, not solely functional superiority.
- Six high-concept senses include Design, Story, Symphony, Empathy, Play, and Meaning.
