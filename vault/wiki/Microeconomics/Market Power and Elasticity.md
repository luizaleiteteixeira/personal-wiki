# Market Power and Elasticity

Market power refers to a firm's ability to influence the market price, which is dependent on the product's uniqueness and the demand curve. The degree of this power can be quantified using elasticity concepts.

## Key points

- Market power is influenced by a firm's ability to affect price, constrained by the demand curve.
- LI ranges from 0 to 1 and quantifies a firm's market power.
- If (p-MC)/P > 1/Ƹ, the price is greater than the competitive price (P*).
- If (p-MC)/P < 1/Ƹ, the price is less than the competitive price (P*).
- A less elastic demand corresponds to a higher degree of market power.
