# Two-Part Tariff Structure

A pricing strategy involving an initial fee and a per-unit price, which allows the seller to capture all the value created in the transaction. This structure is most profitable under specific conditions regarding market knowledge and consumer homogeneity.

## Key points

- The tariff consists of an initial fee (F) and a price per unit (P).
- The initial fee captures the consumer surplus created.
- Profit maximization occurs when the per-unit price equals marginal cost (P = MC).
- The structure is most profitable when customers have identical demand functions and the seller knows this demand.
- The initial fee only works if the consumer's willingness to pay (WTP) exceeds marginal cost (MC).
- Total revenue is calculated as F + P* x Q*.
