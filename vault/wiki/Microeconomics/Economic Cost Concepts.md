# Economic Cost Concepts

Economic cost involves expenditures minus sunk costs and opportunity costs, representing the true cost of making a decision. It is crucial to only consider incremental costs that change based on the decision made.

## Key points

- Economic Cost = Expenditures - Sunk Cost + Opp. Cost
- Only incremental costs that change based on the decision should be allocated.
- Overhead costs are treated as sunk if they will change.
- Average Cost (AC) is total cost divided by quantity (Q).
- Marginal Cost (MC) is the cost to produce one more unit.
- Profit is maximized when Price (P) equals Marginal Cost (MC).
- If P < AC, the firm incurs a negative profit.
- If fixed costs are sunk, the firm should choose quantity Q=0 when P < MC.
