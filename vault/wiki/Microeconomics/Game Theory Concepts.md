# Game Theory Concepts

Game theory analyzes decision-making in situations where outcomes depend on the choices of multiple players. It involves concepts like dominant strategies and Nash equilibrium to predict outcomes.

## Key points

- Strategy is the best response given the belief of other player’s choice.
- Dominant strategy is always the best option regardless of the other player's choice.
- A Nash equilibrium occurs when no player wishes to change their strategy.
- The Prisoner's Dilemma involves dominant strategies leading to a suboptimal outcome for both players.
