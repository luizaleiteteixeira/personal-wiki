# Marginal Cost Estimation

Pricing advisory projects is challenging because accurately measuring the marginal cost is difficult. Decisions cannot rely solely on costs and require judgment and benchmarks.

## Key points

- Overhead costs, such as rent or software licenses, should not be included in the marginal cost.
- The correct measure for marginal cost involves incremental hours and direct expenses like travel.
- Estimating the total cost is difficult due to unknown variables like the time needed to find a suitable candidate.
- Hidden factors like prior experience can affect the time required for the project.
- The opportunity cost of a senior leader's time is hard to quantify without predicting future demand.
- Pricing decisions must incorporate judgment alongside cost analysis.
