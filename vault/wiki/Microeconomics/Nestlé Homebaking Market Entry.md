# Nestlé Homebaking Market Entry

Nestlé successfully identified a shift in consumer behavior during the pandemic, moving demand from out-of-home consumption to home cooking in Brazil. By launching a "Homebaking" segment with specific products, Nestlé became a first mover and gained significant market advantage.

## Key points

- The pandemic caused a shift in Brazilian consumer demand from out-of-home to home cooking.
- Nestlé responded by creating the Homebaking segment and launching products like cookie and brownie dough, chocolate chips, and cocoa powder.
- Early response and branding allowed Nestlé to establish itself as the go-to option for home baking.
- The company marketed its products around themes of family and tradition to build consumer association.
- This strategic move secured market share and consumer trust despite low barriers to entry.
