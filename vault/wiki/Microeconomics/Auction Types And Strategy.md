# Auction Types And Strategy

Auctions involve different payment rules depending on the type, with the optimal bidding strategy varying significantly between first-price and second-price formats. Incomplete information can lead to phenomena like the winner's curse.

## Key points

- In a second-price sealed auction, the winner pays the bid of the second-highest bidder.
- In a second-price auction, bidding one's true value is the dominant strategy if the value is clearly seen.
- In a first-price sealed auction, the best strategy is to bid below one's value to ensure a positive surplus.
- The winner's curse occurs when imperfect information causes the winner to overpay due to an inflated belief about the object's value.
- Ascending auctions are similar to English auctions, and descending auctions are similar to Dutch auctions.
