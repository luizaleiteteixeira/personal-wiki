# Price Elasticity of Demand

Price elasticity measures how sensitive the quantity demanded is to a change in price. It is calculated using the derivative of the demand function.

## Key points

- The slope of the demand curve equals the own price elasticity.
- The unit free elasticity is the change in quantity divided by the change in price.
- If the absolute value of elasticity is greater than 1, demand is elastic.
- If the absolute value of elasticity is less than 1, demand is inelastic.
- Perfect inelasticity occurs when the elasticity is zero.
- In the long run, demand curves tend to be flatter or more horizontal.
