# Simple Monopoly Pricing

A monopolist determines its pricing strategy by understanding the relationship between price, quantity, and revenue. Profit maximization occurs where the marginal revenue equals the marginal cost.

## Key points

- The inverse demand function defines the price P(Q).
- The revenue function is calculated as P(Q) multiplied by Q.
- Marginal Revenue (MR) is the derivative of the revenue function.
- MR represents the change in total revenue from existing and new customers.
- MR has the same intercept and twice the slope as the inverse demand function.
- Maximum revenue is achieved when MR equals zero.
- Optimal quantity (Q*) is found by setting MR equal to Marginal Cost (MC).
