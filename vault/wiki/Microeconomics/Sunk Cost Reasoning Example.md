# Sunk Cost Reasoning Example

Sunk cost reasoning occurs when past, unrecoverable costs influence current decisions. In the example, the initial decision to eat the large pizza led to the feeling of needing to finish it because it had already been paid for.

## Key points

- The money spent on the pizza was already gone, regardless of how much more was eaten.
- Finishing the pizza was driven by the desire to justify the initial payment, not by current hunger.
- Sunk cost mistakes involve taking future actions to rationalize past choices.
- The situation could have been avoided by ordering the small pizza or by focusing on future outcomes.
