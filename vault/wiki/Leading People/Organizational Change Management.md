# Organizational Change Management

Organizational transformation involves adapting structure, strategy, operations, or culture to external pressures. Successful change requires intentional action to overcome natural human resistance to new ideas.

## Key points

- Change can be incremental or radical, involving transformation in structure, strategy, operations, or culture.
- Resistance to new ideas is common, requiring measured and intentional action to manage.
- The three stages of change are Unfreeze, Change, and Refreeze.
- Loss aversion and endowment effect contribute to resistance against change.
- Successful change should be treated as a continuous process embedded in the culture.
- Driving change from the middle out is recommended for successful implementation.
