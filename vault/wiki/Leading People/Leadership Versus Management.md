# Leadership Versus Management

Management focuses on the execution of processes and coordination, while leadership centers on providing vision and influence. Effective high-achievers often possess competencies in both management and leadership roles.

## Key points

- Management involves organizing, coordinating, and executing tasks.
- Leadership involves influencing others and providing a vision.
- Great leaders successfully integrate both management and leadership functions.
- Competence in management can be a prerequisite for becoming a good leader.
