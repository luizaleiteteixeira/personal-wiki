# Authentic Leadership Foundations

Authentic leadership stems from a combination of self-awareness and values-based decision-making. For leadership to be effective, both the leader's internal feeling of genuineness and the external perception of that genuineness by others are important.

## Key points

- Authenticity occurs when a person's behavior mirrors their inner self.
- Experienced authenticity relates to feeling aligned, while perceived authenticity relates to others viewing you as genuine.
- An authentic leader bases their approach on self-awareness and values.
- Impression Management Strategies include self-promotion, ingratiation, supplication, and exemplification.
- The Big Five model (OCEAN) is a highly valid personality assessment tool.
- The five traits are Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, and Neuroticism.
