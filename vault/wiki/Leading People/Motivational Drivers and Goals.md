# Motivational Drivers and Goals

Motivation can be understood through Expectancy Theory, which links effort to performance, performance to reward, and the value of the reward. Sustaining high performance relies on intrinsic rewards, while extrinsic rewards are important for initial engagement.

## Key points

- Expectancy Theory states Motivation = Expectancy × Instrumentality × Valence.
- Specific, challenging, and self-set goals enhance motivation (SMART).
- Extrinsic rewards (like bonuses) are important when commitment to a task is low.
- Intrinsic rewards sustain long-term performance.
- Feedback should be candid, specific, future-focused, and problem-solving oriented.
- Hedonic Treadmill describes the tendency toward ever-rising goals despite achievement.
