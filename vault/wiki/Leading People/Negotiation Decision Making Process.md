# Negotiation Decision Making Process

Negotiation is a decision-making process where two or more individuals agree on how to allocate scarce resources. Successful negotiation involves distinct phases: Prepare, Negotiate, and Finalize.

## Key points

- Positions are the stated requests, while interests are the underlying needs driving those requests.
- BATNA represents your walk-away plan and is a key source of negotiation power.
- The Reservation Price is your minimum acceptable deal, which is informed by your BATNA and opportunity costs.
- The Target Price is a realistic, positive goal you aim to achieve during the negotiation.
- ZOPA is the overlap between the buyer's target and reservation price and the seller's.
- Negotiations can involve distributive issues (win-lose) or compatible issues (mutually beneficial).
- Making an ambitious first offer can anchor the discussion, but going second can be beneficial for gathering information.
