# Group Dynamics And Team Success

Teams can succeed by aggregating information and leveraging diverse expertise, but they can also fail due to homogeneity or groupthink. Effective team functioning requires psychological safety and structured processes to mitigate common pitfalls.

## Key points

- Successful teams aggregate information, divide tasks, and leverage diverse expertise.
- Group failure occurs through homogeneity, deference to power, and quick consensus.
- Groupthink arises from strong cohesion combined with pressure for unanimity, leading to critical thought failure.
- Psychological safety is a shared belief that individuals can speak up, admit errors, and take risks without penalty.
- Structural fixes for decision-making include assigning a dissenter and avoiding early votes.
- Procedural justice relates to the fairness of the process used to reach a decision, not just the outcome.
