# Principles of Persuasion

Persuasion is the act of bringing different parties together toward a shared vision or convincing them to adopt a new viewpoint, which drives motivation. Effective persuasion requires a structured message combined with understanding audience psychology.

## Key points

- The goal of persuasion is to bring disparate parties together or convince them to change, leading to motivation.
- A successful message requires defining the goal, knowing the audience, and building logical and emotional arguments.
- Cialdini's principles include Authority, Scarcity, Social Proof, Liking, Commitment & Consistency, and Reciprocity.
- To use Authority, establish expertise and cite credible sources; to use Scarcity, emphasize limited-time opportunities.
- To leverage Social Proof, show that others are already taking the desired action and share success stories.
- Commitment & Consistency suggests securing a small, public commitment before asking for larger involvement.
- Ethical persuasion aims for a win-win alignment.
