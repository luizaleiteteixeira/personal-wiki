# Social Network Dynamics

Social networks are webs of relationships that facilitate the transfer of information, influence, and opportunities. Leaders leverage these networks to mobilize resources, learn, and achieve goals.

## Key points

- Networks transfer information, influence, and opportunities.
- Expansive networks use weak ties for diverse information and creativity.
- Cohesive networks use strong ties for high trust and rapid consensus.
- Structural holes are gaps between non-redundant contacts, and brokers bridging them hold power.
- Maintaining a network requires gratitude, acknowledgements, favors, and advice.
- Allies bring new information and connections, while opponents allow for productive debate.
