# Inclusive Leadership and Equity

Inclusive leadership involves building systems that allow diverse groups to reach their full potential. This requires moving beyond individual biases to address systemic issues related to fairness and representation.

## Key points

- Diversity is the presence of different identities or characteristics within an organization.
- Equity means striving for fairness, particularly concerning historically marginalized communities.
- Inclusion is the act of cultivating a culture that values respect for all individuals.
- Hiring decisions should be measured rather than based on subjective "fit" to avoid bias.
- Effective DEI policies must be integrated into core business operations, not treated as separate initiatives.
- Building trust requires establishing psychological safety and implementing systems to reduce bias.
