# Organizational Culture Dynamics

Culture is a shared pattern of beliefs, values, and symbols that dictates behavioral expectations within an organization. It provides consistency and meaning while offering a strategic advantage when managed correctly.

## Key points

- Culture is defined by shared patterns of beliefs, values, symbols, and behavioral expectations (norms).
- It provides control through consistent decision rules and acts as a powerful intrinsic motivator.
- Artifacts are the visible expressions of deeper values, such as mission statements or rituals.
- Values are the underlying principles and ideals that guide behavior.
- A strong culture is intensely held and widely shared, but it is not automatically positive.
- Tools like stories, heroes, and ceremonies can be used to manage and reinforce culture.
- A culture that was once an advantage can become a barrier if it does not adapt to a changing environment.
