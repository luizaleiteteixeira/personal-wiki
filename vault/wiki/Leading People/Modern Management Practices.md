# Modern Management Practices

Modern management requires leaders to move beyond automated tasks and focus on uniquely human skills like judgment and coaching. Effective management also benefits from applying rigorous, evidence-based approaches to decision-making.

## Key points

- AI automates coordination, data-processing, and monitoring tasks, shifting managerial value to judgment, ethics, coaching, and meaning-making.
- Google's Project Oxygen identifies eight behaviors of a great manager, including being a good coach and caring about people.
- Evidence-Based Management advocates using systematic evidence over anecdotes and bias, noting that correlation is often mistaken for causation.
- Strong experiments include an independent variable, a dependent variable, a random sample, and a control group.
- Pitfalls in organizational experiments include contamination, small sample size, the Hawthorne effect, and attrition.
