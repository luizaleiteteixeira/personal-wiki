# Jack Rivkin Leadership Approach

Jack Rivkin successfully increased diversity by fostering an inclusive and supportive environment where individuals felt valued and empowered. However, the success of his approach was heavily dependent on his presence, leading to the culture fading when he departed.

## Key points

- Rivkin treated everyone the same and believed in individual potential.
- He cultivated a culture characterized by support, growth opportunities, autonomy, and flexibility.
- The environment had a "no-jerk" policy with zero tolerance for disrespect or bias.
- A drawback was the over-reliance on Rivkin for maintaining this positive environment.
- The culture was not successfully influenced across the entire company structure.
- Improvement would involve institutionalizing these practices across the company's systems and activities.
- This institutionalization requires a top-down commitment from senior leadership.
