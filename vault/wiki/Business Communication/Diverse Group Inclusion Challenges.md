# Diverse Group Inclusion Challenges

Students in a diverse setting faced struggles with inclusion and belonging despite varied backgrounds. Key issues included language barriers, cultural differences, and a lack of psychological safety, leading to communication avoidance.

## Key points

- Students struggled with a lack of inclusion and sense of belonging.
- Language barriers, cultural differences, and fear of misinterpretation were present.
- Silence increased distance and misunderstandings as students feared offending others.
- Homophily occurs when students only interact with those sharing similar backgrounds.
- An inclusive leader must create psychological safety for open conversation.
- Tactical solutions include offering language support and ensuring faculty diversity.
