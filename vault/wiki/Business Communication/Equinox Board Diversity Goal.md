# Equinox Board Diversity Goal

The board should maintain its commitment to achieving a diverse board but adjust how this goal is communicated publicly. The focus should shift from specific group quotas to a skills-based process involving diverse candidates.

## Key points

- The board has already shown performance benefits due to the diversity achieved.
- The goal itself should not be abandoned, but the public framing of it should change.
- Instead of mentioning specific groups, the process can be described as using a skills-based approach with a diverse slate.
- Morrison should acknowledge the original mandate's wording was too strong but not back away from the goal.
- She could use personal experience and performance data to ground the discussion in results.
- The sacrifice needed is in the strict wording of the mandate, not the commitment to diversity.
