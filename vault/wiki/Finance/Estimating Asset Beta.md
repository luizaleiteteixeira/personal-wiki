# Estimating Asset Beta

Beta measures the sensitivity of a stock or project to the overall market. When valuing a project, it is often necessary to estimate the unlevered beta of the real assets, as equity beta alone does not represent the asset risk.

## Key points

- Unlevered beta, free cash flow beta, and asset beta all refer to the same concept: the beta of a real investment.
- Equity is only one of the claims to the project, asset, or firm.
- The unlevered beta can be estimated by treating assets as a portfolio of debt and equity securities.
- To determine project betas, identify comparable firms with similar cash flow risks.
- The unlevered beta is obtained by using the estimated equity betas and debt betas of comparable firms.
- The average unlevered beta is then used in the Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) equation.
