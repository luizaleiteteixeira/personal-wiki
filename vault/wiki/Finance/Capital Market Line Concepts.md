# Capital Market Line Concepts

The Capital Market Line (CML) represents the optimal risk-return possibilities achievable by combining risk-free savings with the Market Portfolio. Beta is a key metric derived from the Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) that measures a security's sensitivity to market risk.

## Key points

- The CML combinations involve risk-free savings and the Market Portfolio.
- The Market Portfolio serves as the benchmark portfolio under the CAPM.
- Beta measures only the common risk of a security, unlike volatility which captures total risk.
- An investment's risk premium equals beta multiplied by the market's risk premium.
- The CAPM relies on three components: the risk-free rate, the project's beta, and the expected market risk premium.
- Beta is the OLS regression coefficient used in this framework.
