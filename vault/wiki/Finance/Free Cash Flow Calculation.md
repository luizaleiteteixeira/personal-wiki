# Free Cash Flow Calculation

Free Cash Flow (FCF) represents the cash flow available to the firm after accounting for its investment needs and taxes. It is calculated by starting with accounting earnings and adjusting for non-cash items, changes in net working capital, and capital expenditures.

## Key points

- Net Present Value (NPV) is the difference between an investment’s market value and its cost.
- Discounting future cash flows accounts for the opportunity cost of invested money.
- Earnings are not cash flows because accruals recognize revenue upon sale, not receipt of money.
- Changes in inventory and accounts receivables require tracking to correctly adjust for cash flows.
- FCFF = EBIT – Taxes – $\Delta$NWC – Capex + Depreciation
- Depreciation is a non-cash expense that impacts cash flow indirectly through taxes.
