# Equity Financing and Firm Value

The announcement of a project immediately increases the equity value of a firm by the Net Present Value (NPV) of that project. Stock prices reflect this NPV upon announcement to prevent arbitrage opportunities, after which the price remains stable.

## Key points

- The issuance price of stock reflects the NPV of the project to prevent arbitrage.
- The value of assets is based on the future cash flows they will generate, excluding upfront costs.
- High leverage acts as a "disciplinary device" to curb managerial overinvestment.
- Overinvestment can occur when cash is abundant, leading to negative NPV projects or excessive spending.
- In bankruptcy proximity, equity holders may gamble for survival, while debt holders bear the loss.
- Highly leveraged firms have an incentive to undertake very risky investments.
